Guardian X-1 Physical Assembly Guide
Phase 1: 3D Printing & Filament Prep
Before assembly, ensure your parts are printed with the correct material specifications to handle thermal, structural, and mechanical loads:

PA6-CF (Carbon Fiber Nylon): Use for high-stress structural parts (motor mounts, folding hinges, and wing arms). Print with 4–5 walls and 35–40% gyroid infill.

CF-PETG: Use for the center_floor, side_rail, and top_hull to maintain dimensional stability and clean aesthetic tolerances (fit_tol = 0.2).

TPU 95A: Use exclusively for the pi5_vibration_mount.scad and rover tyres to absorb high-frequency motor vibrations and ensure ground traction.

Phase 2: Chassis & Core Electronics Integration
Heat-Set Inserts: Using a precision soldering iron set to 220°C, press M3 brass threaded inserts into all designated mounting holes in the center_floor and side_rail modules.

Compute & AI Stack:

Mount the Raspberry Pi 5 (8GB) onto the TPU vibration dampening plate (pi5_vibration_mount.scad).

Attach the Raspberry Pi AI Kit (Hailo-8L) directly to the PCIe slot.

Secure the M.2 HAT and 128GB NVMe SSD underneath the Pi 5.

Flight Stack: Mount the SpeedyBee F405 V4 55A Stack into the center electronics bay using anti-vibration rubber standoffs.

Phase 3: Drivetrain & Aerial Propulsion Installation
Rover Drivetrain:

Install the 4x N20 micro gearmotors into the wheel strut housings, securing them with M3 socket-head cap screws.

Wire the N20 motors to the TB6612FNG motor driver breakout board connected to the Pi/GPIO bus.

Aerial Propulsion:

Bolt the 4x EMAX ECO II 2207 Motors onto the carbon-fiber / PA6-CF folding arms.

Solder motor phase wires directly to the SpeedyBee 4-in-1 ESC.

Sensors & Peripherals:

Mount the RPLIDAR A1M8 on the top deck with a clear 360-degree field of view.

Secure the Arducam ToF Camera and IMX219 1080p Camera to the front fascia bracket.

Position the MLX90640 Thermal Array and HLK-LD2410 Radar inside the front compartment slots.

Phase 4: Power Distribution & Wiring Harness
Power Regulation: Connect the dual CNHL 4S 2200mAh LiPo batteries via an XT60 harness to the SpeedyBee power distribution board (PDB).

Step-Down Rails: Route power from the 5V/12V UBEC regulator to feed the Raspberry Pi 5, Hailo NPU, and auxiliary sensor bus.

UART Link: Connect the SpeedyBee flight controller UART output to the Raspberry Pi 5 GPIO UART pins (Pins 8/10 or /dev/ttyAMA0) at 921600 baud for MAVLink telemetry streaming.

## Step 6: Cellular & eSIM Module Integration
1. **Robot LTE Modem:** Mount the LTE USB/M.2 module into the internal electronics bay. Connect the antenna leads to the external chassis cutouts on the `top_hull.stl` and route the USB serial line to `/dev/ttyUSB2`. Insert the active IoT nano-eSIM.
2. **Smartwatch eSIM:** Solder or slot the wearable nano-eSIM into the smartwatch core PCB during wristband casing assembly to enable remote wearer telemetry over cellular fallback.
